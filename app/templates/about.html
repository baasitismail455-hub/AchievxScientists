WHY THIS EXISTS

More than a list of publications.
A record of the work itself.

AchievX Scientist is designed as a continuously updated research portfolio. Each project can hold its purpose, its people, its progress and the materials generated along the way.

01 / PROJECTS

Give every idea a home.

Describe the question, define the aims, add a visual identity and make the project's current status clear.

02 / PROGRESS

Keep a living record.

Add dated entries as the work evolves. Record experiments, decisions, milestones, observations and next steps.

03 / MATERIALS

Keep evidence close.

Attach datasets, spreadsheets, PDFs, images, audio notes, videos and other project files to the relevant update.

04 / PEOPLE

Work together.

Invite collaborators using their email address. They create a simple account and contribute to the shared project workspace.
